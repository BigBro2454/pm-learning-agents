📌 Archetype 1: Market & News Intelligence Monitor

Solves the enterprise systems problem of runaway token consumption in continuous monitoring. Uses SQLite episodic memory to filter out seen article URLs/hashes, achieving a 90%+ token cost reduction.

Williams' Framework Properties:

  • Autonomy: Moderate — unattended polling loop with pre-defined alerting rules.
  • Reactivity: High — responds dynamically to newly published RSS market signals.
  • Social Ability: Low — unidirectionally dispatches webhook payloads without multi-turn debate.

Operational Mechanics:

  • Perception: RSS XML parser with SHA256 URL hashing.
  • Reasoning: Gemini 2.5 Flash with Pydantic structured output (RelevanceResult).
  • Action: Webhook notification dispatcher (Discord/Slack).
  • Memory: SQLite episodic store (seen_articles table).
